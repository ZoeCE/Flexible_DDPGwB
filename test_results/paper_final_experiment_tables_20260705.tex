\noindent
These paper-ready tables summarize the final controlled-variable descent
evaluation, the highest-difficulty ablation study, and the concentrated
high-wind ablation stress test.  Unless otherwise stated,
all entries use $n=512$ episodes.  Broad success denotes strict insertion plus
lucky insertion; strict success counts only clean insertion.

\caption{Positioning against related work.  The key gap is precision insertion of a flexible cable-suspended payload, where trajectory tracking, rigid peg-in-hole, and pure suspended-load transport do not cover the combined cable--wind--contact setting.}
\label{tab:related_work_positioning}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.16\linewidth}p{0.22\linewidth}p{0.16\linewidth}p{0.20\linewidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
Area & Representative work & Task & Main limitation for our setting & Our positioning \\
\midrule
Cable-suspended RL & FLARE; RoVerFly; CrazyMARL & Agile flight, payload tracking, cooperative transport & No multi-hole precision insertion; contact is not the core objective & We address contact-rich insertion with a flexible suspended payload. \\
Classical suspended-load control & PID/LQR/input shaping/MPC; multi-cable crane insertion studies & Anti-sway transport or single-hole insertion & Strong dependence on model accuracy; limited handling of wind, multi-segment rope, and insertion contact & We keep a simple controller as a stabilizing prior and learn the residual correction. \\
Residual robot learning & Johannink et al.; Residual Policy Learning; ResiP & Contact-rich manipulation or rigid assembly & Usually rigid manipulators or BC/MPC bases, not flexible cable-suspended insertion & We use a domain PID base and learn residuals for cable, wind, and contact mismatch. \\
Rigid/flexible insertion RL & Peg-in-hole RL; force-based assembly; flexible flat cable insertion & Direct insertion with robot arms or deformable cable handling & The manipulator directly applies contact forces; no suspended underactuated payload & Our payload is indirectly actuated through flexible cables. \\
Privileged sensing and encoders & Asymmetric actor--critic; privileged latent policies & Terrain or object-state compression for deployable policies & Mostly locomotion or general manipulation; not 1-D cable-state compression & We compress high-dimensional cable state into a compact learned cable latent. \\

\end{tabularx}

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Core claims and final evidence.  The main comparison uses true observations for all methods, while deployable vision and prediction modules should be reported separately as sim-to-real extensions.}
\label{tab:claims_to_results}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.18\linewidth}p{0.28\linewidth}p{0.27\linewidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
Claim & What must be shown & Final evidence & Paper interpretation \\
\midrule
New task regime & Success should degrade as rope, wind, and precision are added one factor at a time & Traditional controllers collapse from C2--C4, while residual RL remains robust & The controlled ladder isolates the sources of difficulty instead of mixing them in one coarse scale. \\
Hybrid control is necessary & Classical experts and simplified MPC should fail under high model mismatch & At C4, PID/Damped-PD/MPC strict success is $0.0\%$, while residual RL reaches $68.2\%$ strict success & The learned residual is not merely polishing a solved controller; it handles flexible cable/contact/wind mismatch. \\
Cable state matters & Removing cable information should reduce insertion reliability on the hardest setting & On C4, Full 8D reaches $69.5\%$ strict success, while w/o Cable reaches $48.2\%$; at wind $8$--$10$ m/s, strict success is $47.7\%$ vs. $34.0\%$ & Cable information mainly reduces stuck-on-rebar failures and improves clean insertion. \\
Compact cable latent is effective & A larger latent should not automatically outperform the compact one & Full 8D outperforms Full 16D in broad success, strict success, swing energy, and swing angle & The selected trainable 8D cable encoder is a better mainline than simply increasing latent size. \\
Temporal memory refines clean insertion & Removing recurrent memory should reveal whether the recurrent policy is essential or mainly improves insertion quality & The matched 8D MLP reaches $66.6\%$ strict success, while the LSTM reaches $69.5\%$; under wind $8$--$10$ m/s, the LSTM keeps higher strict success & Most capability comes from residual control plus cable state; LSTM slightly improves clean insertion and stability. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
